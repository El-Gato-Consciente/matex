# 🧠 Formalia: The Manifesto of Written Reason

> **"Form must follow function. In mathematics, function is Truth."**

Formalia is born from a fundamental contradiction in modern academia: the fact that mathematical thought—intrinsically pure and structured—ends up trapped in flat, fragile, and semantically poor text files. Writing LaTeX should not be an exercise in defensive programming or a struggle against syntax, but rather the natural culmination of an ordered intellectual process.

We don't just edit documents here. **We compile intentions.**

---

## 💎 The New Paradigm: Truth Resides in the AST

In Formalia, a document does not exist as a simple sequence of characters. It exists as a living **Mathematical Semantic Model**.

* **Beyond WYSIWYG:** While traditional editors attempt to visually "mimic" a PDF, Formalia builds an **Abstract Syntax Tree (AST)** that understands the hierarchy of your definitions, the logic of your derivations, and the ontology of your variables.
* **Representation Independence:** By separating meaning from its visualization, knowledge remains shielded. The AST is the atomic core; the format is merely a projected view.
* **Real-Time Structural Validation:** Forget about unclosed environments or broken hierarchies. The system guarantees the logical integrity of the structure before a single output command is ever generated.

---

## 🏛️ The Forge of Canonical LaTeX

Formalia does not generate code that simply "compiles." It produces **Academic-Grade Professional LaTeX** governed by a rigorous standardization manifesto.

* **High-Precision Typography:** Every generated macro and environment adheres to industry best practices: flawless management of automatic delimiters (`\left/\right`), semantic alignment environments (`align`, `gather`), and surgical control of fine spacing.
* **Deterministic Normalization:** The normalization engine takes user input and processes it under strict typographic rules. The result is source code indistinguishable from the work of a TeX expert with decades of experience.
* **Human-Readable, Elegant Code:** The output is clean, minimalist, and free of unnecessary visual artifacts. It is canonical LaTeX designed to be durable, legible, and fully compatible with any professional editorial workflow.

## 🛠️ Mission-Critical Architecture

Designed for those who demand tools that operate with the same precision as their arguments:

* **MathLive as an Interface of Intent:** Structured visual editing that eliminates friction without sacrificing rigor.
* **Tiptap + ProseMirror:** A node-based document engine designed for extensibility and deep semantic manipulation.
* **TypeScript + Vite Ecosystem:** Next-generation performance and strong typing to ensure a seamless transformation logic.
